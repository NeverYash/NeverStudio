The real problem

A chatbot in every tab is not a workflow.

In most agencies, AI lives in one person's chat history. They found a prompt that works. Nobody else has it. The designer at the next desk solves the same problem from scratch, and the client gets a different voice depending on who was free that day.

More tools will not fix that. A shared way of working will: one tested template per repeated task, one assistant per client that knows the brand, and one checklist that says who signs off.

That is what we build with your team, in the room, on your work.

Straight talk

What we promise, and what we will not.

We promise

  • Your work, not sample exercises. Every session uses tasks from your audit.
  • Everyone builds. Each person finishes real tasks in the room.
  • A person signs off. We teach AI as a first draft and a second pair of eyes, never the final word.
  • Your data rules come first. We agree in writing what may go into an AI tool before day one.
  • Your tools. The method works on the AI tools your team already has.

We will not promise

  • A guaranteed number of hours saved. We measure it with you and report what we find.
  • That AI replaces anyone. It removes repeat work. Judgement stays with your people.
  • Full automation. This training is about how your team works. Automated pipelines are a separate conversation.
  • A wall of agency logos. This is a new programme. We would rather show you the method and let your own numbers speak.

Questions

What agency owners ask first.

Does my team have to stop client work for three days?

They work on client work during the training. The exercises are your real briefs, captions, edits and updates, so deliverables keep moving. That is also why the training stops on Wednesday: Thursday and Friday are normal working days, done the new way.

Which AI tools do we need?

Whatever your team already uses. We teach a method, and it carries across tools. If you have nothing yet, we will suggest a starting point on the fit call.

Is our client data safe?

Before day one we agree in writing which accounts and material may be used, and what never goes into an AI tool. Sensitive work is replaced with made-up examples of the same type. If a client contract restricts AI use, please check it with your own adviser.

What does the full training cost?

It is quoted for your team after the audit, because the scope depends on how many people attend and which tracks you need. The audit itself is a fixed ₹5,000.

Can we do only the audit?

Yes. The written map is yours, and you are free to act on it without us.

We only do content, no video. Is it still useful?

Yes. The content track stands on its own. We shape the three days around the tasks your audit shows.

Will you build automations for us?

Not as part of this training. We focus on templates, assistants and review steps first, because those are what a team can maintain itself.
